# 📊 README — Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

## 🏷️ Project Title

**Retail Sales Performance Dashboard using Power BI**

---

## 📖 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data and developing an interactive business intelligence dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard brings together sales, customer, product, order, category, region, and date-related information to provide a centralized view of business performance.

The project includes data preparation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive data visualization. The final dashboard helps users analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product-wise sales, regional performance, and monthly sales trends.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- 📊 Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- 👥 Understand customer sales information.
- 📦 Analyze product-wise sales performance.
- 🌍 Compare sales performance across different regions.
- 📈 Identify monthly sales trends.
- 🧮 Create meaningful KPIs using DAX.
- 🎚️ Develop interactive filters and slicers.
- 📋 Present business information through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🔄 Methodology / Steps Followed

### Step 1: Data Collection

- Collected the available retail business dataset.
- Identified the required sales, customer, product, category, region, and date information.

### Step 2: Data Import

- Opened Power BI Desktop.
- Imported the required dataset into Power BI.
- Loaded the required tables for analysis.

### Step 3: Data Cleaning and Transformation

- Checked the data for missing and duplicate values.
- Changed data types where required.
- Renamed and prepared columns for analysis.
- Created a Month column from Order Date for monthly sales analysis.

### Step 4: Data Modeling

- Created relationships between the required tables.
- Connected sales information with customer, product, and calendar data.
- Prepared the data model for reporting and visualization.

### Step 5: Dashboard Development

- Created KPI cards for **Total Sales, Total Customers, Total Orders, and Total Profit**.
- Created a **Profit Margin %** KPI.
- Developed a **Monthly Sales Trend**.
- Created **Sales by Category** visualization.
- Created **Profit by Region** visualization.
- Created **Product-wise Sales Analysis**.
- Created **Top 10 Customers by Sales**.
- Created **Quantity by Category and Region**.
- Created **Regional Sales Performance**.
- Added slicers for **Month, Category, and Region**.
- Designed an interactive business dashboard.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 💰 KPI Analysis

The dashboard provides key performance indicators including:

- Total Sales
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %

### 📈 Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart is used to display sales performance across different months and identify changes in sales over time.

### 🏷️ Sales by Category

A doughnut chart is used to compare total sales across different product categories.

### 🌍 Profit by Region

A chart is used to compare sales and profit performance across different regions.

### 📦 Product-wise Sales Analysis

A bar chart displays total sales generated by individual products and helps identify better-performing products.

### 👥 Top 10 Customers by Sales

A column chart displays the top 10 customers based on their total sales contribution.

### 🔢 Quantity by Category and Region

This visualization shows the quantity of products sold across different categories and regions.

### 🌎 Regional Sales Performance

A bar chart compares total sales across different regions.

### 🎚️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard contains interactive slicers for:

- Month
- Category
- Region

These filters allow users to analyze specific parts of the business data.
